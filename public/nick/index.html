Why the current approach is hard

Selling design hours is a tough business right now.

Every client starts as a stranger.

Each project means finding someone, pitching them, and convincing them in a meeting. That's the part nobody enjoys.

The price comparison got brutal.

Owners weigh a custom quote against a $20-a-month site builder and AI tools that promise a site in minutes.

Projects end. So does the income.

A one-time build pays once. Then it's back to finding the next client.

The first 30 days

Prove it by hand before we automate it.

  1. Week 1
    Pick 15 restaurants. Independent Asian spots within a 15-minute drive, with good reviews and weak websites.
  2. Week 2
    Build 15 reports and prototypes. Claude drafts, Nick reviews every one.
  3. Week 3
    Send the invites. Email, plus a printed card with a QR code dropped off or mailed. Track who opens the report.
  4. Week 4
    Count the yeses. Two or three sign-ups means the model works. Then we build the agents to do it at scale.
