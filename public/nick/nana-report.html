Your market

You're in a crowded, well-reviewed neighborhood.

Nana's rank nearby 4th of 9

Your ratings are good, and so are your neighbors'. Within a short drive, diners can pick from dozens of Thai and sushi spots rated 4 stars or higher. When food quality looks the same on paper, people choose based on what they see online: photos, hours they can trust, and how easy it is to order.

Regulars have no way to hear from you

Your hours aren't on the homepage

Nana closes between lunch and dinner on weekdays and serves dinner only on weekends. That split is exactly what people check before driving over, and right now they have to look elsewhere.

What we changed

Five things your new site does.

  1. Shows whether you're open right now. Today's hours sit at the top, including the lunch-to-dinner break.
  2. Puts the food first. Signature rolls lead the page, with clear prices and big photos.
  3. Turns regulars into a list. Nana Rewards: sign up with a phone number, earn a free roll, get first word on specials.
  4. Makes ordering yours. Commission-free pickup ordering under your own name.
  5. Leads with your reviews. Your regulars say it better than any ad could.
